Shop Establishment Registration Assistance

Shop Act registration, also known as Shop and Establishment License, is a mandatory registration for businesses operating in commercial establishments such as retail shops, offices, restaurants, and service centers.

This registration is issued by the labour department or municipal authority and confirms that the business is operating legally under local regulations.

Many small business owners in Moula Ali, ECIL, AS Rao Nagar and nearby Hyderabad areas require Shop Act registration to open bank accounts, apply for GST registration, and comply with labour laws.

Our Shop Act registration assistance service helps business owners prepare the application, verify documents and submit the registration through the official government portal.

Businesses That Need Shop Act Registration

  • Retail shops
  • Restaurants and food outlets
  • Service businesses
  • Offices and commercial establishments
  • Small business startups

Most commercial establishments operating in urban areas must obtain a Shop and Establishment registration certificate.

Documents Required for Shop Act Registration

  • Aadhaar card of business owner
  • PAN card
  • Business address proof
  • Rental agreement or electricity bill
  • Shop photograph

Providing correct documents ensures that the registration application can be processed smoothly.

Shop Act Registration Process

Step 1: Collect business details and documents.
Step 2: Prepare registration application.
Step 3: Submit application through government portal.
Step 4: Shop Act license issued after verification.
