About TS POLYCET

TS POLYCET (Telangana State Polytechnic Common Entrance Test) is conducted by the State Board of Technical Education and Training (SBTET) Telangana. The exam allows students who completed Class 10 to join diploma courses in engineering, technology, and other technical programs in government and private polytechnic colleges across Telangana.

Students who qualify in POLYCET can participate in counselling and choose their preferred polytechnic colleges and diploma courses.
