FSSAI Food License Registration Assistance in Hyderabad

FSSAI food license is a mandatory certification issued by the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India for businesses involved in manufacturing, processing, storing, distributing, or selling food products. Any food-related business operating in India must obtain a valid FSSAI registration or license depending on the size and nature of the business.

Many small businesses such as tiffin centers, bakeries, home kitchens, food stalls, cloud kitchens, and restaurants are required to obtain FSSAI registration before starting operations. The license ensures that the food products sold to customers meet the safety and hygiene standards defined by the government.

Applying for FSSAI license online can sometimes be confusing for new business owners. Incorrect information or missing documents may delay approval or cause rejection of the application.

Types of FSSAI Food Licenses

Basic FSSAI Registration

Basic FSSAI registration is suitable for small food businesses and home kitchens with annual turnover below ₹12 lakh.

State FSSAI License

State FSSAI license is required for medium-sized food businesses such as restaurants, bakeries and food outlets.

Central FSSAI License

Central license is required for large manufacturers, exporters and businesses operating across multiple states.
