Digital Life Certificate Assistance for Pensioners

A Life Certificate, also known as Jeevan Pramaan, is an important document that government pensioners must submit every year to continue receiving pension payments. The life certificate confirms that the pensioner is alive and eligible to receive pension benefits from the government or pension authority.

Earlier, pensioners had to physically visit banks or government offices to submit their life certificate. Today, the Government of India has introduced the Jeevan Pramaan system which allows pensioners to submit their Digital Life Certificate using Aadhaar biometric authentication.

Many pensioners in Moula Ali, ECIL, AS Rao Nagar, and nearby Hyderabad areas prefer visiting a local service center to submit their life certificate because the biometric process requires proper device setup and internet connectivity.

Our digital life certificate assistance service helps pensioners complete the Jeevan Pramaan submission quickly using Aadhaar fingerprint authentication. Once submitted successfully, the certificate is digitally shared with the pension authority.
