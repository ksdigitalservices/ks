Driving License Application Assistance

A driving license is an official document issued by the Regional Transport Office (RTO) that allows individuals to legally drive vehicles on public roads. In India, applicants must first obtain a learning license before applying for a permanent driving license.

Residents in Moula Ali, ECIL, AS Rao Nagar and nearby Hyderabad areas often apply for driving licenses through the Parivahan portal. However, the application process requires document uploads, identity verification and appointment scheduling with the RTO.

Many applicants face difficulties when filling the online forms or selecting the correct license category. Incorrect details or missing documents can delay the application process.
