PAN Card – Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is a PAN card?

PAN (Permanent Account Number) is a unique 10-digit alphanumeric number issued by the Income Tax Department for financial and tax-related transactions.

2. Who can apply for a PAN card?

Any Indian citizen, including minors and businesses, can apply for a PAN card.

3. What documents are required to apply for a PAN card?

Common documents include Aadhaar card, identity proof, address proof, and date of birth proof.

4. How can I apply for a PAN card?

You can apply online through the official PAN application portals or visit a service center that assists with the application process.

5. How long does it take to receive a PAN card?

After successful application and verification, the PAN card is usually delivered within 10–15 working days.

6. Can I apply for a PAN card using Aadhaar?

Yes, PAN can be applied using Aadhaar through an online e-KYC process.

7. What should I do if I lose my PAN card?

You can apply for a duplicate PAN card by submitting a reprint request through the official portal.

8. Can I update details on my PAN card?

Yes, you can apply for PAN correction to update details like name, date of birth, or address.

9. Is PAN card required for bank transactions?

Yes, PAN card is required for many financial activities such as opening a bank account, filing income tax returns, and large financial transactions.

10. Can your center help with PAN card applications?

Yes, we assist customers with PAN card applications, corrections, reprints, and e-KYC submissions.
