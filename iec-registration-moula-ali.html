Import Export Code Registration Assistance

Import Export Code (IEC) is a unique identification number issued by the Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT). Businesses and individuals who want to import or export goods from India must obtain an IEC code.

Many entrepreneurs in Moula Ali, ECIL, AS Rao Nagar and nearby Hyderabad areas apply for IEC registration to start import-export businesses or sell products internationally.

IEC registration allows businesses to legally import goods into India and export products to international markets.
