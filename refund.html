If KS DIGITAL SERVICES is unable to fulfil the agreed work or provide the requested service due to a reason within our control, you may be eligible for a refund of the amount paid for the unfulfilled service.

When refunds are not applicable

How to request a refund

If we are unable to fulfil the agreed work, contact KS DIGITAL SERVICES with your payment reference, registered mobile number and a brief description of the issue. We will review the request and communicate the outcome.
