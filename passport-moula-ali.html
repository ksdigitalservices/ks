Passport Application Assistance in Moula Ali

A passport is an official travel document issued by the Government of India that allows citizens to travel internationally. It also acts as an important proof of identity, address, and nationality. Many individuals require passports for employment abroad, higher education, tourism, immigration, or business travel.

The passport application process includes online registration, filling the passport application form, uploading documents, paying fees, and attending biometric verification at Passport Seva Kendra (PSK). After biometric verification, police verification is conducted before the passport is printed and dispatched.

How to Apply Passport Online in India

Applicants must register on the Passport Seva portal and complete the application form online. After submitting the form, applicants must pay the passport fee and book an appointment at Passport Seva Kendra. During the appointment, biometric verification and document verification are conducted.

Step 1: Register on Passport Seva portal
Step 2: Fill passport application form
Step 3: Upload required documents
Step 4: Pay passport fee
Step 5: Book PSK appointment
Step 6: Biometric verification
Step 7: Police verification and passport delivery

Indian Passport – Frequently Asked Questions

1. Who can apply for an Indian passport?

Any Indian citizen can apply for a passport by submitting the required documents and completing the verification process.

2. What documents are required for a passport application?

Common documents include Aadhaar card, PAN card, voter ID, birth certificate, address proof, and passport-size photographs.

3. How can I apply for a passport in India?

You can apply online through the Passport Seva portal, fill out the application form, pay the fee, and book an appointment at the Passport Seva Kendra.

4. How long does it take to get a passport?

The processing time usually ranges from 7 to 30 days depending on police verification and document approval.

5. What is police verification in passport application?

Police verification is a process where local police confirm your identity and address before the passport is issued.

6. Can I apply for a passport without a birth certificate?

Yes, in some cases alternative documents such as school certificates or government-issued age proof can be used.

7. What should I do if my passport application is rejected?

You can reapply after correcting the issue or submitting the required documents mentioned in the rejection notice.

8. Can I correct mistakes in my passport?

Yes, you can apply for passport reissue to correct errors such as name, address, or date of birth.

9. How long is an Indian passport valid?

For adults, a passport is generally valid for 10 years, while for minors it is usually valid for 5 years or until they turn 18.

10. Can your center help with passport applications?

Yes, we assist customers with filling the passport application, uploading documents, paying fees, and booking passport appointments.
