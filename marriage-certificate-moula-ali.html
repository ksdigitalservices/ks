Marriage Certificate – Frequently Asked Questions

1. Who can apply for a marriage certificate?

Any legally married couple can apply for a marriage certificate. The marriage must have taken place according to legal or religious customs.

2. What documents are required for a marriage certificate?

Required documents usually include Aadhaar cards of both husband and wife, wedding photos, address proof, age proof, and identity proof of witnesses.

3. Is the presence of both husband and wife required?

Yes, in most cases both husband and wife must be present during the application or verification process.

4. How many witnesses are required?

Generally two witnesses are required for marriage registration. They must provide identity proof such as Aadhaar card.

5. How long does it take to get a marriage certificate?

The processing time usually ranges from a few days to a few weeks depending on the local municipal office and verification process.

6. Can we apply for a marriage certificate online?

Yes, the application can often be submitted online through the government portal, but verification may still require visiting the office.

7. Can we register a marriage that happened years ago?

Yes, marriages performed earlier can still be registered, but additional verification or documents may be required.

8. What if there is a mistake in the marriage certificate?

If there is an error in the certificate, a correction request must be submitted along with supporting documents.

9. Is a marriage certificate required for passport or visa?

Yes, many passport, visa, and legal procedures require a valid marriage certificate as proof of marital status.

10. Can your center help with the marriage certificate application?

Yes, we assist customers with filling the application, uploading documents, and submitting the marriage certificate request online.
